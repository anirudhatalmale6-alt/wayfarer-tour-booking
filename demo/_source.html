Places to visit

What there is to see

Ladakh is bigger and emptier than most people expect. These are the places worth the drive, and roughly what each one costs you in time.

2 days from Leh

Pangong Tso

134km of lake at 4,350m, two-thirds of it in Tibet. The colour changes hour to hour. Stay the night — day trips waste it.

2–3 days from Leh

Nubra Valley

Cold desert with real sand dunes at Hunder, the Shyok river, and Diskit's 32m Maitreya looking north.

Half day

Thiksey & Hemis

Twelve storeys of white-and-ochre monastery above the Indus. Morning prayers at Thiksey start before sunrise — worth the alarm.

Half day

Khardung La

5,359m and one of the highest motorable passes anywhere. Ten minutes at the top is plenty on your first days.

4–7 days

Zanskar Valley

Cut off by snow half the year. Rafting on the Zanskar in summer, and the frozen river walk in January.

1–2 days

Leh old town

Nine-storey palace, the bazaar, Shanti Stupa at sunset, and the two days of acclimatising you should not skip.

What we carry, what you carry

On homestay routes you carry a daypack and nothing else. On camping routes, ponies carry the tents, kitchen and your main bag; you walk with water, layers and a camera.

  • Guide, cook and pony support on all camping treks
  • Two-person tents, mats, mess tent and toilet tent
  • All meals on trek, hot drinks morning and evening
  • First aid and a pulse oximeter with every group

Travel insurance covering trekking to your route's maximum altitude is a condition of joining. We will tell you the figure to insure for.
